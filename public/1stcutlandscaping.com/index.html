Home / About

Local groundskeeping out of Mount Pearl

1st Cut Landscaping operates from 59A Smallwood Dr in Mount Pearl, NL. We provide reliable, scheduled lawn care, garden maintenance, seasonal clean ups, and winter snow clearing for residential homeowners and local businesses across the Northeast Avalon.

Newfoundland properties face specific seasonal demands: heavy spring thatch after freeze-thaw cycles, acidic soil that requires dolomitic liming, wind exposure on newly planted trees, and wet snowfalls that pack tightly at the end of driveways. Our crews equip for all four seasons so property owners can rely on one local contact year-round.

Common questions from local homeowners

Practical answers regarding soil care, mowing schedules, online quoting, and winter snow clearing on the Avalon Peninsula.

Because coastal rainfall makes Avalon soils naturally acidic, moss tends to crowd out grass if soil pH drops too low. We apply pelletized dolomitic lime in spring after power-raking or in early autumn, followed by slow-release fertilizer once soil temperatures warm up in May.
When you submit your street address and selected services on our Estimate page, we measure your turf area, driveway dimensions, and frontage using municipal parcel imagery. For straightforward lawn care, clean ups, and snow clearing, we send a written quote by email within one business day (Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM). If your project involves full sodding or heavy tree planting, we schedule a brief on-site check.
During peak growth from late May through July, weekly cutting keeps grass healthy because we never remove more than one-third of the blade height at once. Bi-weekly cutting works well for shaded lots or drier stretches in August.
